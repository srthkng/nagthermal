Our Team

The Multiscale Thermal Engineering Laboratory (MTEL) is a place where we explore exciting problems in heat transfer and fluid mechanics. Our work mainly focuses on electronics cooling, battery thermal management, and next-generation thermal technologies. Using a mix of experiments and simulations, we enjoy tackling real-world engineering challenges and turning ideas into practical solutions.
