News

Stay updated with the latest activities from the Multiscale Thermal Engineering Laboratory. (Last Updated: 28.08.2026)

2026

August 2026

Amit Verma submits M.Tech. (R) thesis

We are pleased to share that Amit Verma, M.Tech. student, has submitted his M.Tech. thesis. His thesis, titled “Thermal-Hydraulic Analysis of Mini-Channel Structures for Proton Exchange Membrane Fuel Cells (PEMFCs) Bipolar Plate Designs,” focuses on the thermal and hydraulic performance of mini-channel structures for PEMFC bipolar plates, contributing to the development of efficient thermal management solutions for fuel cell systems.

August 2026

New M.Tech. (R) Student joins MTEL

We are pleased to welcome Avutala Sushanth Reddy to the Multiscale Thermal Engineering Laboratory (MTEL). He will be working on Battery Thermal Management Systems as part of our research on advanced thermal management technologies.

July 2026

Research paper published on advanced cold plate cooling for data centres

We are pleased to share that Abhinandan Bhardwaj, Ph.D. Scholar, has published a research article in collaboration with Dr. Randeep Singh, Fujikura, on advanced cooling technologies for data centres. The study, titled “Computational study on the thermo-fluidic performance of manifold microchannels with circular and square pin fins in cold plates for data centre cooling,” investigates the thermo-fluidic performance of manifold microchannel cold plates. The work has been published in Thermal Science and Engineering Progress, Volume 77, Article 104853 (2026).

July 2026

Sarthak visits Kyushu University & Fujikura Ltd. (Japan)

Dr. Sarthak Nag visited the Department of Mechanical Engineering, Kyushu University, Japan, hosted by Prof. Masamichi Kohno, from 3–25 July 2026. The visit provided an opportunity for academic interactions, research discussions, and exploring potential collaborations in the areas of thermal and fluid engineering. During the visit, Dr. Nag also visited the Research and Development facilities and Headquarters of Fujikura Ltd. to engage in discussions on emerging research areas and explore opportunities for future research collaboration.

June 2026

MTEL Alumni receive degrees at the 14th Convocation of IIT Mandi

We are delighted to share that three MTEL alumni — Dr. Anil Pundir, Basudev Mahato, and Amit Singh — received their degrees at the 14th Convocation of IIT Mandi. Anil Pundir was awarded his Ph.D. degree, with his doctoral research co-supervised by Prof. Arpan Gupta, IIT Delhi. Basudev Mahato and Amit Singh received their Master’s degrees. We congratulate all three alumni on this significant milestone and wish them continued success in their future endeavours.

June 2026

Amit Singh defends M.Tech. (R) thesis

We are pleased to share that Amit Singh, M.Tech. (R) student, successfully defended his M.Tech. thesis on 4 June 2026. His thesis, titled “Thermal-Hydraulic Performance of Triply Periodic Minimal Surface (TPMS) Heat Sink,” focuses on the thermal and hydraulic performance of TPMS-based heat sinks for forced-air thermal management of power electronics. We congratulate Amit on successfully completing his M.Tech. research and wish him continued success in his future endeavours.

March 2026

Abhinandan Bhardwaj selected for JST-LOTUS exchange programme

We are pleased to share that Abhinandan Bhardwaj, Ph.D. Scholar, has been accepted as an exchange student under the JST-LOTUS programme at the Department of Mechanical Engineering, Kyushu University, Japan. His exchange period is from 19 March to 31 October 2026, during which he will undertake research and academic activities at Kyushu University. As part of the exchange, he will be working on cold plate designs for advanced thermal management applications, furthering his research in next-generation cooling technologies.
